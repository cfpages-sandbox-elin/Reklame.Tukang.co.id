## Position and boundary

The site should help brand owners, property teams, agencies, and buyers brief, compare, procure, install, operate, and maintain physical/digital advertising displays. It does not provide structural/electrical design, permit or tax rulings, traffic-safety approval, copyrighted artwork, or unsupported audience/performance claims.

## Evidence gates

- Dimensions, loads, wind, foundations, anchors, lifting, electrical protection, fire, traffic visibility, luminance, IP rating, structural capacity, and public safety require current standards, product data, permits, and competent project design.
- Permit, placement, zoning, owner consent, and advertising-tax guidance must be verified for the exact jurisdiction, site, format, size, and date.
- Brightness, energy, reach, impressions, colour, life, warranty, lead time, and price claims require measured or dated like-for-like evidence.
- Artwork, fonts, logos, photography, and third-party property require documented rights.
- Damaged, unstable, energized, overhead, or roadside displays require isolation and qualified assessment; no DIY repair guidance.
